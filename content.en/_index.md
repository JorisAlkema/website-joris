**Joris D.C. Alkema**

{{< home-photo alt="Joris Alkema on a forest hiking trail." >}}

I am a Research Associate at Leiden University, where I work in the Network Science Group within [PLANET-NL](https://www.planetnl.org/), a collaboration with the University of Amsterdam. Supervised by Prof. Frank Takes, I work on making population-scale social network data, based on restricted-access microdata from Statistics Netherlands (CBS), available for research without exposing the individuals in it.

Alongside this, I work part-time as a Platform Engineer (MLOps) at Braincreators, where I build and maintain the company's Kubernetes platform, CI/CD pipelines and ML infrastructure.

I hold an MSc in Computer Science from Leiden University. My MSc thesis led to my first paper, [*Fast and Effective Search for Budgeted Network Anonymization*](/en/blog/network-anonymization/), which has been accepted at [COMPLEX NETWORKS 2026](https://complexnetworks.org/). My BSc thesis studied [the effect of clock glitches on neural networks](/en/blog/clock-glitches/) running on embedded hardware.

Outside work I run and swim, both with more enthusiasm than speed. More in my [CV](/resume.pdf).
